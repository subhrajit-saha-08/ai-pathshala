## Brand & Style

This design system channels youthful curiosity, optimism, and warmth. Built for engaging educational and school environments, it establishes a supportive, delightful digital ecosystem where learning feels joyful, approachable, and rewarding.

The aesthetic blends **Playful Tactility** with **Luminous Soft Modernism**. It avoids clinical software austerity by embracing organic warm gradients, friendly pill-shaped accents, pillowy elevation, and tactile, candy-coated touchpoints. The visual atmosphere evokes golden hour sunlight reflecting off vibrant creative tools: luminous blush coral-pink paired with radiant buttery yellow-gold, grounded by calming periwinkle-lavender neutrals.

## Colors

The palette radiates friendliness and high-energy encouragement while preserving legibility and accessibility across all age tiers.

- **Primary (`#FF4D6D`)**: Vibrant Coral-Pink. Drives key calls-to-action, success celebration markers, and focal interactive elements. Supported by deep rose (`#C9184A`) for active states and pale blush (`#FFE5EC`) for contextual surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#FFB703`)**: Radiant Sunny Amber/Citrus Gold. Channels playfulness, rewards, streak counters, and creative highlights. Supported by soft buttercup (`#FFF3BF`) for soft alert fills and high-satisfaction badges.
- **Tertiary (`#8486E8`)**: Soft Periwinkle/Lavender. Offers calming balance against warm pigments, applied to delicate outlines, secondary tabs, informational indicators, and tranquil surfaces.
- **Neutral (`#4A4058`)**: Deep Warm Slate-Plum. Avoids harsh carbon blacks in favor of an organic, ink-like deep purple-slate that ensures WCAG AAA typography contrast on crisp surfaces.
- **Backdrop Canvas**: Ambient mesh gradients transitioning smoothly from delicate sunrise blush (`#FFF0F5`) through radiant soft butter (`#FFFDF0`) to gentle periwinkle mist (`#F3F2FE`).

## Typography

The design system uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all levels. Its balanced geometric construction, generous counters, and gentle organic terminals convey approachability and clarity without sacrificing reading rhythm.

- **Headlines & Display**: Set with heavier weights (700 and 800) and slight negative tracking to produce tight, punchy headers that feel buoyant and celebratory.
- **Body Content**: Employs comfortable line heights (1.5x–1.6x) and balanced weights to support young readers and busy educators during prolonged reading sessions.
- **Labels & Microcopy**: Distinctly weighted with slightly expanded tracking for instantaneous legibility on pills, badge counters, and interactive buttons.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column fluid grid for desktop and tablet, collapsing to a 4-column system on mobile devices.

- **Canvas & Structure**: Ample outer margins create an open, airy feeling reminiscent of an open sketchbook. Containers float inside radiant atmospheric backdrops without crowding page boundaries.
- **Spatial Rhythm**: The 8pt vertical rhythm guides internal component spacing. Generous spacing tokens (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) separate modular learning activity cards and dashboard panels to prevent cognitive overload.
- **Responsive Adaptations**:
  - **Desktop (>=1024px)**: 12-column grid, 24px gutters, generous 32px–48px page margins.
  - **Tablet (768px–1023px)**: 8-column grid, 20px gutters, 24px margins.
  - **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column fluid stack, 16px gutters, 16px margins with edge-to-edge swipeable carousels for card decks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **Ambient Tinted Shadows** combined with **Soft Translucent Glass Underlays** instead of dark neutral drop shadows.

- **Surface Base**: Crisp, pure white (`#FFFFFF`) card surfaces provide sharp contrast against luminous gradient backdrops.
- **Tinted Ambient Elevation**:
  - **Low Lift (Cards, Inactive Elements)**: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(132, 134, 232, 0.08), 0 2px 6px 0 rgba(255, 77, 109, 0.04)`.
  - **Medium Lift (Hover States, Interactive Panels)**: `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(255, 77, 109, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -1px rgba(255, 183, 3, 0.08)`.
  - **High Lift (Modals, Floating Action Pods)**: `0 20px 40px -6px rgba(74, 64, 88, 0.14), 0 8px 16px -2px rgba(255, 77, 109, 0.12)`.
- **Tactile Edge Outlines**: Elevated surfaces feature a 1px soft periwinkle-to-white translucent rim (`rgba(132, 134, 232, 0.18)`), creating defined silhouette edges without visual harshness.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Pill-shaped (Level 3)** design language. Curves are soft, generous, and inviting to interaction.

- **Small Controls & Chips**: Full pill radii (`9999px`) provide playful tactile affordances.
- **Cards & Modules**: Bound by extra-large radii (`rounded-xl: 3rem`, `rounded-lg: 2rem`), reinforcing comfort and safety across educational touchpoints.
- **Input Fields & Buttons**: Fully curved pill shapes create cohesive, stamp-like interactive surfaces that invite tapping and clicking.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Buttons**: Vibrant coral-pink (`#FF4D6D`) fill with crisp white text, full pill curvature, and subtle warm drop shadow (`rgba(255, 77, 109, 0.35)`). Active state applies a micro-scale bounce (`scale: 0.97`) and deep rose wash (`#C9184A`).
- **Secondary Buttons**: Radiant butter-yellow fill (`#FFD166`) with deep plum text (`#4A4058`), providing high-energy alternative actions.
- **Tertiary / Ghost Buttons**: Pure white translucent base (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`), soft lavender border (`#8486E8`, 1.5px), and lavender text.

### Chips & Badges
- Continuous full-pill geometry (`rounded-full`).
- **Status Tiers**:
  - *Sunny Progress*: Pastel butter yellow background (`#FFF3BF`) with amber text (`#B26A00`).
  - *Cheer Pink*: Warm blush background (`#FFE5EC`) with deep coral text (`#D90429`).
  - *Calm Periwinkle*: Gentle periwinkle background (`#E8E9FC`) with deep indigo-lavender text (`#4346A2`).

### Cards & Content Pods
- Rendered in solid white (`#FFFFFF`) or frosted glass (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)` with 16px blur) set against the ambient backdrop.
- Enclosed with soft periwinkle borders (`1.5px solid rgba(132, 134, 232, 0.2)`).
- Generous internal padding (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) and corner curves (`rounded-xl`).

### Form Inputs
- Fully rounded pill containers with pure white fill and light periwinkle border outlines (`1.5px solid #D6D7F8`).
- **Focus State**: 2px ring in radiant coral (`#FF4D6D`) accompanied by a diffuse coral ambient glow (`0 0 0 4px rgba(255, 77, 109, 0.15)`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Circular checks and pill toggles. Checked states fill with vibrant coral (`#FF4D6D`) displaying crisp white checkmarks. Unchecked states feature soft periwinkle-tinted boundaries.

### Additional Product Components
- **Progress Trackers & Quest Meters**: Pill-shaped capsule tracks filled with a sunset gradient (coral-pink transitioning to radiant gold) with smooth spring-based fill animations.
- **Floating Reward Badges**: Circular or pill reward tokens with 3D-like multi-tiered warm ambient shadows and celebratory starburst iconography.
